Replace low-quality leads with qualified sales conversations.

AtlasDemand is a new outbound service for B2B SaaS founders. Every message starts with research on one company and goes to one named person there.

What every message needs

Company
It looks like your best customers: the same kind of business, the same size, the same problem.
Person
One person, by name, who owns the decision. No shared inboxes.
Reason
Something public and recent about the company, such as a hire, a launch or a funding round.

Each batch starts small. The replies decide who gets the next one.

Who it is for

B2B SaaS companies in the US with roughly 10 to 200 people.

Why did I get an email from you?

I choose companies from public information, such as a funding round, a leadership hire or a sales job posting, and I write each email about one fact from it. I find work email addresses on public pages and through business-contact data providers. If you would like to know where I found yours, write to me and I will tell you.

I send one short email. If you do not reply, I may follow up at most twice, then I stop.

To stop, reply “unsubscribe” to any of my emails. I will not write to you again, and I keep your address on a do-not-contact list so it is never used again.
