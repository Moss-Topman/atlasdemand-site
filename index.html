Find the right accounts. Identify the right people. Know why they’re worth approaching.

I’m building AtlasDemand to help B2B SaaS companies research potential customers, identify relevant decision-makers, and prepare personalized outreach based on company-specific evidence.

I don’t just compile lists of businesses. I investigate why a company may be relevant to your product and give your sales team a researched reason to approach it.

What I do

1. Company research

I identify companies that match the customer profile agreed with your team.

I examine:

  • Industry and business model.
  • Company size and growth stage.
  • Target markets.
  • Customer profile.
  • Potential relevance to your product.

A company’s similarity to your existing customers is a starting point, not proof that it has the same problem.

2. Buyer identification

I identify people relevant to your sales approach, including:

  • Founders and CEOs.
  • Heads of Sales.
  • Revenue and growth leaders.
  • Relevant department heads.
  • Other decision-makers and influencers.

I focus on relevant professional contacts and use business information from public pages and data providers where available.

3. Evidence and buying signals

I investigate publicly available company information that could make an account relevant.

Examples include:

  • Sales and leadership hiring.
  • Product launches.
  • Funding announcements.
  • Market expansion.
  • New partnerships.
  • Changes in company strategy.

I distinguish verified information from reasonable inferences and hypotheses.

A public company development is a reason to investigate, not proof that the company needs your product.

What you receive

Each research batch will include:

  1. A prioritized list of potential customer accounts.
  2. Company profiles and ICP-fit assessments.
  3. Relevant decision-makers and business contact information.
  4. Supporting research and source references.
  5. Potential problems and relevant buying signals, with confidence labels.
  6. Personalized outreach drafts for selected accounts.

How I work

  1. Understand your customer profileI learn about your product, target market, ideal customers, and the problems your solution is designed to address.
  2. Identify suitable companiesI research and select accounts that match the agreed customer profile.
  3. Investigate accounts and buyersI examine each company, identify relevant contacts, and investigate why approaching the account might make sense.
  4. Prepare personalized outreachI connect available evidence to your product and prepare relevant messages for identified contacts.
  5. Review and refineI begin with an agreed, manageable batch. Your feedback helps me improve account selection and research for subsequent batches.

Who it is for

My initial focus is US-based B2B SaaS companies with approximately 15 to 75 employees, particularly founder-led teams that rely on outbound sales to reach identifiable business buyers.

What AtlasDemand does and does not do

The initial service covers account research, buyer identification, account prioritization, and personalized outreach preparation.

Your team retains control of approving and sending messages, managing replies, following up, and running its sales process.

Campaign execution, reply management, and appointment booking are not included in this initial service.
